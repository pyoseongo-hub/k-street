In autumn, Namsan shows a different face.
A free guide in 12 languages, no sign-up — link in bio.

It sits in the middle of the city. But walk the path a while
and the noise falls away, until all you hear is leaves
under your feet.

Every place opens in KakaoMap or Naver Map — the apps that
actually give directions here. The Korean name is there too,
to show a driver.
