## 자세한 소개 (4,000자 한도)

### 영어

```
K-Street is a free guide to Seoul's neighborhoods, made for visitors.

Most guides send you to the same handful of places. This one starts from the
neighborhood: pick a district, or pick a season, and see what is actually on.

WHAT IS IN IT
· Neighborhood festivals, month by month, across all 25 districts
· Traditional markets
· Flower walks and hiking trails
· Museums and cultural places
· Where to leave your luggage, including the subway's own storage

TWELVE LANGUAGES
English, 日本語, 中文 (简体 · 繁體), 한국어, Tiếng Việt, ไทย, Bahasa Indonesia,
Español, Français, Deutsch, Русский.

BUILT FOR ACTUALLY GETTING THERE
· Every place opens in KakaoMap or Naver Map — the apps that work in Korea
· Korean names and addresses are kept as they are, so you can show them to a
  taxi driver or paste them into a map
· Works offline once a page has loaded — useful underground

NO SIGN-UP, NO ADS, NO TRACKING
There is no account. There are no adverts. There is no analytics of any kind.
If you tap "My location", your position is used on your phone only and is never
sent anywhere.

WHERE THE INFORMATION COMES FROM
Places and photos come from the Korea Tourism Organization's open data.
Subway luggage storage prices and locations come from Seoul Metro's own pages.
Each page shows the date the information was checked.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
It does not list opening hours or prices for private shops, because those change
and a wrong number leaves you standing outside with a suitcase. It tells you what
to ask instead.
```

(약 1,400자)
